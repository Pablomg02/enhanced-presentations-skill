Sum of a geometric progression

Obtain the sum of the first n terms of a geometric progression
Definition of the sum of the first n terms
Both sides are multiplied by the ratio r
When the two expressions are subtracted, the intermediate terms cancel in pairs
Sn and 1 − r are factored out, and the sum is solved for

Example: final capital with compound interest

2,000 € is deposited at a compound interest of 3 % per year.

Compute the final capital after 10 years.

Data
Find

Approach: necessary expressions

Data C0 = 2,000 € · i = 3 % per year · n = 10 years    Find C10
For C10
Each year the capital is multiplied by the factor (1 + i): interest is computed on the accumulated capital
The rate is used in decimal form before substituting
Order of computation: convert the rate to decimal form and substitute into the compound interest expression

Solution: final capital

1
Cn: final capital with compound interest
C0, i and n are substituted; inside the parentheses, 1 + 0.03 = 1.03
The factor (1 + i)^n accumulates the interest of the ten years
Each year the interest is added to the capital and generates new interest

Capital growth

  • The capital grows in a multiplicative way, not linearly
  • In year 10 the accumulated factor is 1.34
  • With a higher rate the curve separates quickly

What prevents an airplane from climbing higher?

A passenger airplane flies at about 11 km; the International Space Station, at about 400 km.

Hint:what does the engine need besides fuel?

Answer

The higher it climbs, the less air there is: the wings provide less lift and the engine runs out of oxygen.
